The immediate outcome was not merely a military skirmish but a geopolitical turning point: Japan's rapid advance across mainland China led to the capture of major population centers, most notably Nanjing (then Nanking). Historical estimates place the death toll in the hundreds of thousands over weeks, transforming what was a military objective into one of profound human tragedy and international moral crisis. The brutality demonstrated that Japan’s aims were not simply conquest but the subjugation and dehumanization of the local population.
