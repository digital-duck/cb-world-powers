Economic Instability Extremism

Economic instability extremism describes the mechanism by which acute or prolonged economic distress—such as hyperinflation, mass unemployment, or rapid wealth inequality—undermines established social trust and middle-class stability, creating a fertile ground for the radicalization of fringe political ideologies. The core process is not merely that poverty causes revolution; rather, it involves the systematic erosion of institutional legitimacy and the replacement of complex economic problems with simple, scapegoating narratives (e.g., blaming an external group or "global elites"). This breakdown of trust shifts political focus from structural reform to radical, often anti-systemic, solutions.
