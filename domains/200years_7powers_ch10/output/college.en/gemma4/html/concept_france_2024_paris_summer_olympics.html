France 2024 Paris Summer Olympics

The hosting of the 2024 Paris Summer Olympics was, in geopolitical terms, far more than a sporting spectacle; it represented a meticulously managed national performance intended to project stability and cultural resilience. The mechanism at play is that of soft power projection: using universal cultural assets to temporarily eclipse deeper political fissures and reaffirm national identity on a global stage.

To understand this function, one must look beyond the medal counts. Consider the juxtaposition of the massive, globally focused spectacle—parades, international media saturation, state-of-the-art infrastructure—against the backdrop of persistent domestic protest movements. While the IOC mandated an atmosphere of celebration and order, French authorities had to manage visible labor unrest and political dissent throughout the preparatory years. This tension reveals the core application: The Olympics did not solve France's political fragmentation; rather, they provided a high-visibility framework through which the state could organize its image—a façade of flawless competence masking underlying societal stress.
