The revisionist_powers_pattern is the natural endpoint because it is not one signal but a synthesis — it integrates military posture, economic coercion, alliance behavior, information operations, and legal argumentation into a single coherent read on state intent. A state making one unusual move (a naval exercise, a tariff, a treaty withdrawal) is ambiguous in isolation.
